The MongoDB Community replica set {{ include "mongodb-community.fullname" . }} is being deployed by the MongoDB Controllers for Kubernetes. Watch its status with:

  kubectl get mongodbcommunity --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "mongodb-community.fullname" . }} -w

If the status stays empty, the operator does not watch the namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} (see `operator.watchNamespace` in the README).
